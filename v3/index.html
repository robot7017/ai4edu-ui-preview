这次实验 · 首发场景

PCR

以教师可锁定的标准流程为起点，关注多步移液和试剂匹配中的关键事件。

首版优先验证可观察事件与过程支持价值，不将最终扩增结果等同于平台效果。

  • E1试剂、样本或反应管错配

Why

Many lab mistakes only show tens of minutes later.

Every bench moves at its own pace and one teacher can’t watch them all; by the time a result looks wrong, the faulty step is gone.

The experiment · first use case

PCR

Begin with a teacher-controlled protocol and key events in multi-step pipetting and reagent matching.

The first release prioritizes observable events and process support; amplification outcomes alone do not measure platform effectiveness.

  • E1Reagent, sample or tube mismatch
  • E2Pipette model, range or setting mismatch
  • E3Missed tip changes or insufficient evidence
  • E4Missing or misplaced control tubes
  • E5Omitted or duplicate additions
  • E6Prolonged stalls and repeated help requests

One full experiment

In this PCR class, a student goes through four scenes.

01Inspiration02Instrument use03AR lab work+Teacher view

01Inspiration

It starts with a question.

On the tablet, students pick a scenario and build their own PCR plan with Dr. Pipette guiding.

  1. Pick a scenario
  2. State your question
  3. Ask Dr. Pipette
  4. Find the target segment
  5. Design primers and groups
  6. Draft a PCR protocol

02Instrument use

Learn the instruments first.

Instrument training is its own stage: master the micropipette step by step before the bench.

  1. Choose the pipette
  2. Set the volume
  3. Hold it correctly
  4. Feel the two stops
  5. Fit the tip
  6. Prepare and aspirate

03AR lab work

Put on the glasses, step up to the bench.

The glasses see the action, match it to the protocol step, and prompt in view when something is off.

  1. See · Find the pipette and its dial
  2. Align · Item 1/9 · water 20 µL
  3. Check · Reads 201.6 µL, out of range
  4. Prompt · Turn the dial down; tablet updated

+Teacher view

Teachers see every bench.

Deviations at the benches gather on the teacher view: see which bench needs help, then replay the key events.

  1. Benches needing attention turn red
  2. Open a group to see its key events
  3. Replay events after class
